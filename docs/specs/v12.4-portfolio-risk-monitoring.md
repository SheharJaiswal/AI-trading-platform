# V12.4 — Portfolio Risk Monitoring

## Scope
Deterministic, paper-only portfolio risk observation for concentration, gross exposure, and drawdown.

## Rules
- Risk monitoring is advisory/observational and never submits orders.
- Position concentration defaults to 25% of equity.
- Gross exposure defaults to 100% of equity.
- Drawdown alert defaults to 20% from peak equity.
- Missing prices fall back to average entry price; invalid peak equity produces no event.
- Thresholds are validated before evaluation.

## Acceptance
A portfolio can be evaluated deterministically and produces typed severity events for breached thresholds. Unit tests cover concentration, drawdown, and invalid configuration.
