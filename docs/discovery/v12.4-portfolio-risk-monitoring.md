# V12.4 Portfolio Risk Monitoring

## Scope

Evaluate portfolio equity, peak equity, position market values, concentration, gross exposure, and drawdown using explicit configuration.

## Safety

This component is observational and advisory. It does not submit, cancel, or modify paper orders and cannot bypass deterministic trading risk controls.

## Events

- Position concentration
- Gross exposure
- Drawdown

Thresholds are configuration values; no business threshold is hard-coded as a product policy.
