# V3 UI Requirements

## Primary navigation

- Dashboard
- Research
- Portfolio
- Alerts

## Dashboard

Show paper-mode status, cash, portfolio value/P&L, open positions, and recent alerts.

## Research

Provide symbol input, current quote, recommendation, technical evidence, timestamp/provenance, AI research, risks and thesis invalidation.

## Portfolio

Provide positions with quantity, average entry, current price, unrealized P&L and stop loss. Selecting a position opens its detail and alerts.

## Trade confirmation

The user must explicitly confirm a paper trade. The confirmation view shows symbol, quantity, expected price, recommendation, risk decision and any warning. A blocked decision cannot be submitted.

## UX states

Every data surface needs loading, empty, stale, insufficient-data, risk-blocked and error states where applicable.

## Safety UX

Paper-only execution must be visible. AI-generated text must be visually and semantically separated from deterministic risk authorization.
