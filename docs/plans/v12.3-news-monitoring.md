# V12.3 News Monitoring

Implement confirmed FR-04 news intelligence as an observation layer. Preserve publication time and affected instruments where available and classify recency, sentiment, and materiality. Do not select or require an external provider in this slice.

Safety: news is advisory evidence only. It cannot create, modify, or close orders and cannot bypass deterministic risk validation.

Non-goals: production news vendor selection, automatic trading, automatic liquidation, external notification channels, market-hours assumptions, portfolio-risk thresholds.

Acceptance: publication timestamp retained when supplied; stale/current status deterministic and configurable; sentiment and materiality explicit; affected symbols optional; tests cover stale/current boundaries and missing metadata.
